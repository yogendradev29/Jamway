RANTHAMBORE SAFARI RATES

Choose your safari.

Popular safari options for guests exploring Ranthambore. Availability and final pricing are confirmed for your selected dates.

POPULAR

Gypsy / Jeep Safari

₹2,300–₹2,600 / Indian guest

Open 4×4 safari, suitable for smaller groups and wildlife photography.

  • Up to 6 guests
  • Approx. 3–3.5 hours
  • Permit + vehicle + guide commonly included
  • Availability varies by zone and slot
BEST VALUE

Canter Safari

₹1,300–₹1,800 / Indian guest

Open-top shared safari bus for families and larger groups looking for a budget-friendly option.

  • Up to 20 guests
  • Approx. 3–3.5 hours
  • Guide + permit commonly included
  • Zone access depends on allocation
PRIVATE

Private Gypsy

₹15,000–₹20,000 / vehicle

Private vehicle for guests who want their own Gypsy for a family or photography-focused outing.

  • Up to 6 guests
  • Private vehicle allocation
  • Guide / permit charges generally arranged with booking
  • Exact tariff depends on date and booking type
