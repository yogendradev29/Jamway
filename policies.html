BOOKING INFORMATION

Policies & conditions.

A simple guide to reservations, stays, dining and safari arrangements.

00

Reservations

Availability requests are subject to confirmation. A reservation is treated as confirmed only after the booking details, applicable tariff and required confirmation have been accepted.

01

Rates & inclusions

Room rates and package rates can vary by date, occupancy, room category and selected meal plan. The confirmed booking communication will state the applicable rate and inclusions.

02

Check-in & check-out

Check-in and check-out timings are communicated with the reservation details. Early check-in or late check-out may be subject to availability and additional charges.

03

Cancellation & changes

Cancellation, modification and no-show conditions depend on the selected rate or package. The applicable conditions should be checked before the reservation is confirmed.

04

Guest information

Guests may be asked to provide valid identification at check-in. Please provide accurate guest and contact details when making an enquiry.

05

Children & extra guests

Please mention the number and ages of children and the total number of guests. Any extra-person or bedding charges will be confirmed with the booking.

06

Dining

Meal inclusions follow the selected plan. Dietary preferences, special meals and packed meals should be requested in advance and remain subject to availability.

07

Safari & activities

Safari permits, zones, vehicles, timings and guides are subject to availability and the rules of the relevant authorities. Wildlife sightings cannot be guaranteed.

08

Pets

Pet stays should be requested before booking. Acceptance and any applicable conditions depend on the property and accommodation selected.

09

Property conduct

Guests are expected to respect the property, staff, other guests and local wildlife. Damage to property or misuse of facilities may result in applicable charges.

10

Personal belongings

Guests are responsible for their personal belongings. Please use any safety facilities provided by the property and contact staff if assistance is required.

11

Privacy

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to enquiries, reservations and service requests. It should not be used for purposes unrelated to the guest enquiry.
