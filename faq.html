HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about stays, dining, safari experiences and enquiries.

How do I check room availability?+

Choose your check-in and check-out dates on the Resort page, select the number of rooms and guests, then send the request. The booking team can confirm availability and the applicable rate.

Can I ask for a safari while booking my stay?+

Yes. You can choose a safari package and send the dates, guest count and any special requirements through the enquiry form.

What dining options are available?+

The dining section covers breakfast, lunch and dinner, with Indian, Rajasthani and continental-style choices. Special meals, packed meals and selected dining experiences can be requested in advance.

What is included in the room rate?+

The inclusions depend on the selected room and meal plan. The final inclusions and rate are confirmed with your booking request.

Can I arrange a private celebration or event?+

Special occasions, private meals and celebrations can be discussed with the property in advance. Share your requirements with the enquiry team.

Can children stay at the property?+

Families are welcome to enquire. Please include the number and ages of children so the appropriate room and meal arrangements can be discussed.

Can I bring a pet?+

Pet arrangements should be confirmed before booking because acceptance can depend on the accommodation and the stay dates.

What is the best time to visit Ranthambore?+

Ranthambore can be visited across the season, while wildlife sightings, weather and safari availability vary. The safari team can help you plan around your dates.

Are safari bookings guaranteed?+

Safari permits, zones, vehicles and forest access are subject to availability and park rules. A request is confirmed only after the applicable arrangements are confirmed.

How do I contact the property?+

Use the enquiry buttons or the contact number shown throughout the website. Your request can include dates, guests, room requirements and special remarks.
